# 2026-11-25: B19, the graph of a past lobby ends with the evening

- Before: the BAC timeline was always worked out from the lobby's first drink
  up to the current time. A lobby from last week gave a graph one week wide
  with the evening squeezed into its left edge, "2h" and "5h" showed flat lines
  at zero, and the work (one step per ten minutes, per member, per drink) grew
  with the lobby's age and was redone every 30 seconds.
- Now: the timeline ends at the first 10-minute step at which every member has
  absorbed all their drinks and is back at zero, and never later than now
  (`BACCalculator.timelineEnd`). `simulateTimeline` got a `stopsWhenSober`
  switch for this; without it, it behaves as before.
- During an evening nothing changes: somebody still has alcohol left, so the
  end is "now". Once everybody is at zero the graph stops growing to the right.
  "2h" and "5h" then show the last two or five hours of the evening, because
  the graph window was already measured from the last point.
- The values in "Live BAC" are unchanged: after the end of the timeline
  everybody is at zero by definition.
- `LobbyView.swift` is not touched, so this does not meet #51 or #52.
- Tests: `BACTimelineEndTests` (past lobby, running evening, "now" between two
  steps, the member who is sober last decides, members without drinks, no
  drinks, a drink dated after now, same values as the full simulation, and two
  snapshot tests). The existing `BACCalculatorTests` are unchanged.
- Verified: build and unit tests in CI. Not tested on a device.
- Human check:
  - Open a lobby from a few days ago from "Your Lobbies": the graph shows the
    evening over its full width, and "2h" shows its last two hours.
  - In a running lobby with drinks in the last hour: the graph ends at the
    current time as before, and the values in "Live BAC" look as before.
- Backlog: B19 ticked. Only the checkbox line is changed, so the tick does not
  collide with the other pull requests of today.
